---
title: Where is my data stored?
categories: [faq-main]
permalink: /misc/faqs.html
---

Wherever you want: ProjectForge runs on your own server, in your own data center or behind your own firewall, and keeps everything on your own server: database, file store and search index.
So you decide who has access to customer data, salaries and invoices – which makes GDPR compliance a matter of your own policies.
Nothing goes to a cloud of ours or of third parties, there's no telemetry, and the code is open source: that's digital sovereignty, without vendor lock-in. More in the [user guide](/docs/userguide/#data_sovereignty_and_gdpr).
