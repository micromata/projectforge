---
title: Where is my data stored?
categories: [faq-main]
permalink: /misc/faqs.html
---

Wherever you want: ProjectForge runs on your own server, in your own data center or behind your own firewall, and stores everything in your own PostgreSQL database.
So you decide who has access to customer data, salaries and invoices – which makes GDPR compliance a matter of your own policies.
