---
title: Is the chat end-to-end encrypted?
categories: [faq-main]
permalink: /misc/faqs.html
---

The confidential conversations are: for a password or access data, two people exchange texts that are encrypted in one browser and decrypted only in the other. The server relays them without being able to read them and stores nothing – not in the database, logs, backups or mails.
The normal messages of rooms and direct messages are stored on your own server, so that they can be read later and searched; only the participants see them, administrators don't. More in the [user guide](/docs/userguide/#chat_confidential_conversations).
