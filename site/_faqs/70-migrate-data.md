---
title: We're using spreadsheets and other tools today. How do we get our data in?
categories: [faq-main]
permalink: /misc/faqs.html
---

ProjectForge imports a lot of data you already have: DATEV accounting records and chart of accounts (the Excel file from your tax advisor), incoming invoices from a DATEV CSV export, bank account statements, salaries from Excel, and external calendars.
For everything else – customers, projects, orders or timesheets from your current tools – we're happy to help as part of our [professional services](/services/).
