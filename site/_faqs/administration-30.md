---
title: How do I get my logo into the PDF outputs?
categories: [administration,faq]
permalink: /misc/faqs.html
---

Put your logo file into `resources/images` of the ProjectForge home directory and configure it in `projectforge.properties` (`projectforge.export.logoFile`). See [Logo and title in pdf outputs](/docs/adminguide/#logo_and_title_in_pdf_outputs) in the administration guide.
