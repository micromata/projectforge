**Community support:** post your questions, bug reports and wishes as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/micromata/projectforge/issues). We read them – after all, we're using ProjectForge every day ourselves.

**Professional support:** do you need fast response times, help with installation, updates or interfaces? Ask us for [professional services](/services/) tailored to your company.
