title: Is there a mobile app?
categories: [faq-main]

Yes: the [ProjectForge app for iPhone and iPad]({{ site.app_store_url }}) is free in the App Store, an Android version will follow. It books time sheets, shows the chat with push notifications, the addresses and business cards, and opens every page of the web interface. It connects only to your own ProjectForge server ([features of the app](/features/#mobile_app)).
