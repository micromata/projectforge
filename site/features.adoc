ProjectForge connects orders, projects, timesheets, people, invoices and liquidity. Every feature works on the same data, so what your team books today shows up in tomorrow's forecast – without exports, imports or spreadsheets in between.
All features are part of the free open-source edition. Your users only need a web browser.

[[projects_orders]]
== Projects & Orders

_Know every order, every budget and every project's status._

Order book:: Offers and orders with positions, budgets, payment schedules and any number of contact persons. ProjectForge tells you when something is ready to be invoiced – and when an order is about to be over-invoiced.
Structure tree:: Structure customers, projects and phases in an unlimited hierarchy. Decide per group and task who may see, manage and book what.
Project wizard:: Create a new project with its tasks, groups and access rights in one go.
Consumption bars:: See at a glance which tasks are on budget and which are running hot.
Resource planning:: Plan your people across projects week by week, so project leads and employees always know who works on what.
Gantt charts:: Visualize your plan for your team and your customers.

[[time_tracking]]
== Time Tracking

_Booking time takes seconds, not a Friday afternoon._

Timesheets with templates:: Book recurring work from your favorites with a single click.
Book from the calendar:: See your bookings next to your appointments, or book several days at once – holidays and weekends are skipped automatically.
No double bookings:: Overlapping bookings are prevented – unless you explicitly allow them for shared costs.
Reports and statistics:: Monthly employee reports and personal statistics for employees, project leads and payroll.
Bulk edit:: Select many bookings and change them at once.

image::2026-Timesheets.png[Timesheets,link=/uploads/screenshots/2026-Timesheets.png]

[[people_leave]]
== People & Leave

_Leave requests, leave accounts, employees and skills – without an extra HR tool._

Leave requests:: Request leave, get it approved, everybody involved is notified. Conflicts within the team are detected early.
Leave becomes a timesheet:: Approved vacation is booked as timesheets automatically every night.
Leave accounts:: Entitlements, carry-overs and remaining days – always correct.
Employees and salaries:: Employee data and salaries, importable from Excel and exportable to DATEV.
Skill matrix:: Who knows what? Your team rates its own skills and interests – a great help when staffing new projects.

image::2026-Calendar.png[Team calendar with leave and holidays,link=/uploads/screenshots/2026-Calendar.png]

[[finance_e_invoicing]]
== Finance & E-Invoicing

_From booked hours to a compliant invoice – XRechnung and ZUGFeRD included._

Outgoing invoices:: Invoice positions linked to orders and projects, Word templates for your layout, PDF output and cancellation invoices.
E-invoicing:: Create PEPPOL-ready XRechnung and ZUGFeRD invoices, checked for completeness before export – and check the e-invoices you receive with the built-in e-invoice checker.
Incoming invoices:: Capture or import incoming invoices and assign them to cost units.
Cost accounting:: Cost units and accounts give you clean results per project, customer and department.
Currencies:: Amounts in foreign currencies with online exchange rates.

image::2026-Invoice.png[Outgoing invoices,link=/uploads/screenshots/2026-Invoice.png]

[[controlling_liquidity]]
== Controlling & Liquidity

_Know today what next quarter looks like._

Forecast:: Expected revenue month by month, compared with previous years, as Excel export.
Key figures and charts:: Order statistics, contribution margins and invoicing quota on chart tiles you can arrange the way you like.
Liquidity planning:: Expected payments, recurring series and invoices combined into one liquidity forecast.
Bank accounts:: Import account statements and browse the transactions of your accounts.
DATEV:: Import accounting records and incoming invoices from DATEV and get business assessments (BWA).

[[collaboration]]
== Collaboration

_Team calendars, address book, file sharing and Word templates – the everyday things your team needs anyway._

Team calendars:: Shared calendars with holidays and birthdays. Subscribe to them on any device via ICS and show external calendars inside ProjectForge.
Address book:: Shared addresses, synchronized to your phone via CardDAV, vCard export.
Data transfer:: Share files with colleagues and customers – protected by password and expiry date, with a full activity log.
Word templates:: Generate contracts and serial letters from your own Word templates, as Word or PDF.
Polls:: Ask your team and get a quick answer.
Office organization:: Contracts, incoming and outgoing mail log, visitor book, library, software licenses and hardware.

Modern interface:: Fast and responsive – on the desktop, tablet and phone. In a light or dark theme.

[[security_integration]]
== Security & Integration

_Fits into your IT, not the other way round._

Single sign-on:: Keycloak or Authentik, or LDAP – as client or as master of your users and groups (including POSIX and Samba accounts).
Two-factor login:: Authenticator apps, WebAuthn security keys, codes by mail or SMS.
Fine-grained rights:: Groups, roles and access rights per task.
External gateway:: Offer calendars, contacts and file sharing to the outside world without exposing your main system.
Integrations:: Access tokens for REST clients and calendar subscriptions, phone integration (Sipgate direct call, SMS), Jira issue links.


[[operations_extensibility]]
== Operations & Extensibility

_Easy to run, easy to extend._

Docker or JAR:: Runs anywhere Java runs, with PostgreSQL in production. Database migrations run automatically on update.
System dashboard:: Problems, statistics, scheduled jobs and caches in one place – plus an error digest by mail.
Log viewer:: Live log and full log-file search with context, right in the browser.
Scripting:: Kotlin and Groovy scripts for your own reports and exports – even as buttons on selected pages.
Plugins:: Extend ProjectForge with your own modules.
Open source:: Kotlin, Spring Boot and Next.js. Read the code, change it, contribute.

image::2026-SystemDashboard.png[System dashboard with the scheduled jobs,link=/uploads/screenshots/2026-SystemDashboard.png]

[[requirements]]
== Technical requirements

- Server: Docker image or executable JAR (Java 21 or newer) for Linux, macOS and Windows, see link:/docs/installation/[Installation].
- Database: PostgreSQL for production, an embedded database for evaluation.
- Users: a current web browser – nothing to install on the client.
