## 2. This website

projectforge.org is a static website. We don't collect, store or analyse any data about visitors:

- no access logs of our own,
- no cookies,
- no web analytics, tracking or advertising,
- no contact form, no comments, no newsletter,
- no social media plugins and no content loaded from third parties: fonts, scripts and images are served by the website itself.

To remember the language switch, the website uses the session storage of the browser. This value stays in the browser,
is never sent to us and is deleted when the browser tab is closed.

## 3. Hosting

## 4. External links

The website links to other websites, e.g. GitHub, Docker Hub and SourceForge (downloads). Once a link is followed, the
privacy policy of that provider applies; we have no influence on it.

## 5. ProjectForge app

ProjectForge is self-hosted: every organization runs its own ProjectForge server (on premises or with a hoster of its
choice). The ProjectForge app for iOS and Android works only with this server:

- The app connects only to the server whose address the user enters. It never communicates with us.
- We don't receive or collect any data from the app: no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking, no advertising.
- The data shown and entered in the app (e.g. time sheets, addresses, chat messages) are processed only on the
  organization's server. The controller for these data is the organization that runs the server; questions about them go
  to its administrators.
- Camera and photo library are only used when the user attaches a photo or video to a chat message; it is sent to the
  organization's server only.
- The login data are stored encrypted on the device (Keychain/Keystore).
- Links to maps, websites, phone numbers or e-mail addresses open the corresponding app of the device (e.g. Apple Maps,
  browser); their privacy policies apply.

The app is downloaded through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). These stores process data of their own
(e.g. for the download and the account) according to their privacy policies; we don't receive any personal data from
them.
