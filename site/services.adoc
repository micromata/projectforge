Everything on this page is optional and offered on request. The software itself stays free and complete, whether you book our services or not.

== What we offer

Hosting:: We run your ProjectForge instance, install updates and take care of backups and monitoring.
Introduction:: We help you set up your structure tree, groups and access rights, cost accounting and leave management, so you can get started quickly and on solid ground.
Data import:: We bring over customers, projects, orders, timesheets or accounting data from your current tools and spreadsheets.
Integration:: We connect ProjectForge to LDAP, Keycloak or Authentik, your mail server, DATEV workflows or your phone system.
Support:: Response times and contact channels that fit your business.
Extensions:: Your own reports, scripts, plugins or features – developed by the ProjectForge team.

== Who we are

ProjectForge is developed by https://www.micromata.de[Micromata], a software company in Kassel, Germany.
We have used ProjectForge to run our own company every day since 2001.
